Six services, one panel

Each service is a thin, opinionated layer over infrastructure that already handles deliverability, routing, and registration at scale — not a rebuild of any of it.

Email

Mailboxes customers can drop into Outlook

Create an address, and the panel hands back host, port, and security settings for standard IMAP/SMTP setup — plus inbound mail capture and sending, right from the dashboard.

Voice

Numbers registered straight to a SIP endpoint

Search by country and area code, provision a number, and get SIP credentials your customer enters into any IP phone or softphone — via a dedicated trunk per account.

Voice

A call centre on top of your numbers

Build IVR menus, route callers into queues, and ring your agents' real phones — "press 1 for sales" without a separate PBX to run.

Private network

Self-hosted calling, zero carrier

A private SIP registrar for your own team's IP phones — internal calling with no telecom carrier involved, managed from the same dashboard.

Domains

Search, price, and register domains

Real-time availability and locked pricing, a deliberate multi-step confirmation before anything is charged, and DNS record management once a domain is live.

Development
